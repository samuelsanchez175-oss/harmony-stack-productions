FAQ

Straight answers before you download.

Is Penwork Studio free?

Yes — free on the App Store for iPhone (iOS 16 and later). Download and start writing without a subscription wall for the core studio.

What can I track on each song?

BPM, key, scale, syllable counts, titles, lyrics, and timestamps — so your catalog stays searchable and session-ready.

How do AI line suggestions work?

AI features are bring-your-own-key: you add a provider key on device, then requests go from your device to that provider. Harmony Stack doesn’t middleman your prompts. See Terms & Privacy for details.

Who owns my lyrics?

You do. Your content stays yours. We don’t claim ownership of your songs or compositions.
