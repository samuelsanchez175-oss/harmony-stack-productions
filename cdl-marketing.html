Is it really only $1?

Yes — a one-time App Store purchase. No weekly subscription, no “Pass Pro” upsell after you start. You pay once and study.

Are there ads?

No. Free competitors often interrupt practice with ads. CDL One Stop is ad-free so you can stay in the zone between loads or before bed.

Does it work in my state?

Yes. All 50 states + Washington DC, with Class A, B, and C filtering. Practice is aligned to standard CDL knowledge areas; always confirm local DMV rules and handbook updates.

Will questions match the real DMV test?

Practice questions follow the same topics and style as the commercial knowledge exam (General Knowledge, Air Brakes, endorsements, etc.). No app can guarantee identical official wording — use this to master the material, then walk in ready.

Yes. English y Español — including state licensing strings — so more drivers can prep in the language that sticks.

You can start studying without building a full account wall. Optional Sign in with Apple is available for sync when you want it. Progress is designed to stay on your device by default.
