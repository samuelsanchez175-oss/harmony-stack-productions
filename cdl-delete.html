User data deletion

Delete your CDL One Stop data

This is the official user-data deletion page for the CDL One Stop app (Apple App Store and Meta / Facebook ads). Use it to wipe study progress on your device and to request deletion of any account we hold.

Effective August 14, 2026
1

Delete everything on your device

Open CDL One Stop → Profile → Account → Delete Account. Type DELETE to confirm.

That permanently wipes the User row on this device: test sessions, answers, badges, streaks, key dates, and module progress. There is no undo.

You can also delete the app itself. That removes all local CDL One Stop data from the device.

2

Delete a signed-in account (cloud)

If you signed in with Sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we also hold an account identifier and (when you provided it) an email address with our authentication provider.

Email harmonyStack@gmail.com from that same address and ask us to delete your account. Write from the address on the account so we can verify it is you.

  • We delete the account and associated records.
  • We confirm by email when it is done.
  • We aim to finish within 1–2 business days (sooner when we can).
3

Things we do not store ourselves

  • App Store purchase — Apple holds billing. Refunds go through reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Game Center — scores and achievements are Apple’s. Manage them in Game Center / your Apple Account.
  • Calendar events — delete those events in Calendar, and revoke calendar access in iOS Settings if you want.

We do not sell your data. Study progress stays on your device unless you signed in. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Request account deletion

Send one email from the address on the account. A real person handles it.
