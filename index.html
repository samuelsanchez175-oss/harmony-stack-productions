FAQ

Straight answers before you write.

Who is Harmony Stack?

A solo design-and-build practice focused on websites, Shopify stores, and apps (including AI-assisted products). You work directly with the person building the work — not a handoff chain.

Do you only do websites?

No. Websites and stores are the core, but shipped work also includes iOS apps (like CDL One Stop and Penwork Studio), web apps, and LLM/AI feature integration.

How do payments work?

Projects are quoted as flat rates within the package ranges above. Typical flow is deposit to start, balance before launch — exact terms are confirmed in writing for your project.

What do I need to provide?

For most builds: brand assets (logo, colors, fonts), images, copy (or a copywriting add-on), and access to domain/hosting or platform accounts. A kickoff checklist is shared after we agree on scope.

Can you take over an existing site?

Yes, including Shopify and WordPress sites. Quick Fix and Refresh are built for existing sites; full rebuilds and new stores are Starter through E-Commerce. Share your platform and URL in the inquiry form.

Where are you based?

U.S.-based and remote. Email harmonystack@gmail.com or call (201) 745-7995.

Who owns the site or code after launch?

You do. Deliverables and access are handed over at launch. I don’t hold your domain or codebase hostage.

Do you write content or shoot photography?

Copywriting is available as an add-on. Photography is usually client-supplied; I can recommend structure and help place assets. Product photos for stores should be high-res when possible.

What if I only need a landing page?

That’s a common Starter or smaller custom scope. Say “landing page only” in the form — you’ll get a quote that matches the real work, not a forced 10-page package.

How long until we start?

Depends on the queue. Many projects start within 1–2 weeks of deposit; rush delivery is available as an add-on when the calendar allows.

How feedback works.

Send one list per round. Mark up screenshots and add a line on what you’d like.

01

Take Screenshots

Go through the full preview carefully. Screenshot every area you want changed. Use Snipping Tool, Command+Shift+4 on Mac, or your phone.

02

Mark Them Up

Circle, arrow, or annotate the exact element on each screenshot. Use Canva, Google Slides, iPhone Markup, or any annotation tool. Vague screenshots cause delays.

03

Write Full Context

For each screenshot, write a clear explanation of what's wrong and what you want instead. "Make it bigger" is not enough. Reference a site or describe exactly what you mean.

04

Send One Complete List

Compile everything into one message. Do not send revisions one at a time over multiple days. Review the full site, collect all changes, then send them together.

One pass per revision round. Sending changes in pieces uses up rounds fast. Collect every change you want, review the entire site, then submit everything together in a single list. Rounds can’t be split or carried over, so a single list per round keeps things moving.

What I’ll ask you for

Before the project clock starts, send one delivery: Part 1 (brief below) + Part 2 (Dropbox, logins, checklist).

Part 1

Creative direction & requirements

Put this in a doc or email — not Dropbox.

What to include: 2–4 reference sites (what you like or want to avoid); page list / sitemap; draft copy per page or a clear plan (copywriting add-on $100–250/page if you need drafts); form details (where submissions go, fields, booking/CRM). Clearer references usually mean fewer revision rounds.

Optional: Shopify apps, payments/shipping, chat or popups, pixels, platform preference — note what you already pay for.

Part 2

Assets, access & permissions

Use the cards for brand & images, then the hosting grid and checklist for domain, hosting, and platform access.

Cards marked Required must be complete for kickoff. Upload files through Dropbox — see Dropbox section — not email. Optional lines can follow without blocking the clock.

How to Organize Your Dropbox Folder

All assets submitted via Dropbox. Invite harmonystack@gmail.com with edit access. Build folders as page → section → files (diagram below).

Like macOS Finder column view: each column is one folder level — click folders below to drill in; the next column shows what’s inside. Drop files in the matching folder in real Dropbox (e.g. hero images in hero/). Swap folder names for your sitemap anytime. Number page folders (01, 02…) to match your sitemap.
